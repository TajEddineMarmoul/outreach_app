# Natural Writing & Outreach Guidelines

When writing any cold emails, outreach messages, templates, or communication to recruiters, managers, or peers, follow these core rules:

---

## 1. Eliminate AI Punctuation & Robotic Formatting
- **No unnecessary hyphens:** Do not use artificial hyphenations like `5-min`, `ultra-short`, or `high-converting` in copy. Write `5 min` or `5 minutes`. Real people do not type compound hyphens in casual business messages.
- **No em dashes (`—` or `–`):** Use simple commas, periods, or clean line breaks.
- **No excessive symbols:** Avoid underscore dumping `__`, heavy parentheses, or overly rigid bullet trees.
- **Natural tone:** Write how a real, busy professional actually speaks and types.

---

## 2. Keep It Short & Scannable
- **Length:** Under 50 to 75 words total (3 to 4 sentences max).
- **Time to read:** 5 seconds on a phone screen without scrolling.

---

## 3. Zero Fluff & No Robotic Habits
- **No fake praise or generic openers:** Never say *"I saw that [Company] is expanding..."*, *"I hope you are well"*, or canned compliments.
- **No buzzword stuffing in parentheses:** Avoid dumps like `(Docker/K8s/AWS)`. Mention 1 or 2 core areas in plain text.
- **No resume pasting in the message body:** Don't cram bullet points or metric essays into the text. The CV exists for that.
- **No unnatural phrasing:** Never say *"my 1 page CV"* or *"as per attached"*. Simply say *"I've attached my CV for your review"*.
- **No link clutter:** Maximum of one link (e.g. LinkedIn only).

---

## 4. Low-Friction Call to Action (CTA)
- Do not ask for phone or calendar time on first contact (no *"Can we do a 5 min chat?"*).
- Ask simple, low-effort questions that can be answered in 2 seconds:
  - *"Do you have any open roles on your team where this might be a fit?"*
  - *"Would this be relevant for any current openings on your team?"*

---

## 5. Gold Standard Example

```text
Subject: Backend Engineer / Taj-Eddine Marmoul

Hi [Name],

I'm a software engineer working primarily with Python and cloud systems, and I'm interested in engineering opportunities at [Company].

I've attached my CV for your review.

Do you have any open roles on your team where this might be a fit?

Best,
Taj-Eddine Marmoul
[LinkedIn URL]
```
